Natural-calamity risk estimator

Pick a place and a month. See how often floods, heat and strong wind happened there in the past 35 years, and how to prepare.

Statistical estimate from ERA5 reanalysis (~25 km grid), not an official forecast. Check IMD / local authorities for warnings. Nobody can forecast daily weather for a future year. This tool shows how often extremes happened in that calendar month in past years, and whether they are trending.

Which part of the area is more affected?

      

      

      

      
Lower elevation raises flood risk, because water collects in low ground. Points are directions from the geocoded centre. The "near …" names come from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) for each point's exact coordinates; a point's name does not mean the whole neighbourhood is affected.
